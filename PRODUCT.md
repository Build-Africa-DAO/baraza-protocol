# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Community founders, admins, and members across African groups use Baraza to set up shared treasuries, pay dues, and govern together without needing blockchain knowledge. They are usually working from a phone-first context, often with mixed digital literacy, and need the flow to stay clear, trustworthy, and fast.

## Product Purpose
Baraza helps communities define shared rules, move money into a community treasury, and coordinate membership and voting through one guided experience. Success means a founder can understand the setup path quickly, complete the first critical steps with confidence, and move into creating or joining a real community without friction.

## Brand Personality
Grounded, dignified, and practical. The product should feel warm and local without becoming ornamental, and it should communicate confidence, clarity, and collective ownership.

## Anti-references
Do not make Baraza feel like speculative crypto tooling, a generic Western SaaS dashboard, or a fintech app that foregrounds chains, wallets, and jargon. Avoid noisy gradients, gimmicky motion, or UI patterns that make trust-sensitive flows feel experimental.

## Design Principles
1. Keep the chain invisible and the community legible.
2. Earn trust through clarity, restraint, and explicit next steps.
3. Prioritize phone-first completion for real-world onboarding conditions.
4. Make setup feel actionable, not ceremonial.
5. Preserve momentum from first question to first useful outcome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, keep tap targets large enough for mobile use, support reduced-motion preferences, and use direct language that works for mixed experience levels. Core actions should remain understandable without prior wallet, crypto, or governance knowledge.
