## PART B: Plain-Language Explainer

### The Stellar Community Fund (SCF) Ladder: Three Tiers

Baraza's primary non-dilutive capital source is **Stellar's SCF**, a 3-tier funnel designed to move builders from idea → product → scale.

#### Tier 1: Instaward (~$5K)
- **Who can apply:** Projects pre-approved by local Ambassador Chapters (e.g., Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda)
- **What you need:** 30-day scope, clear deliverable
- **Timeline:** ~4 weeks from approval to payout
- **Baraza status:** ✅ Completed (Jun 2026)
- **Live link:** https://communityfund.stellar.org/

#### Tier 2: Build Award (up to $150K)
- **Who can apply:** Any builder. Two-stage process:
  1. Submit interest form (30 lines max, idea + team)
  2. If selected, invited to submit full proposal
- **Funding tracks:** 
  - *Track A (Classic):* up to $50K for DAO/treasury tooling
  - *Track B (Ecosystem):* up to $100K for infrastructure
  - *Track C (Growth):* up to $150K for adoption/retention (highest bar)
- **Payout structure:** Tranches tied to milestones (typically 3 tranches over 6–9 months)
- **Timeline:** Interest forms typically due every 6 weeks; two-stage process takes 8–12 weeks
- **Baraza status:** 🔴 Interest form due **16 Aug 2026** (Deadline: 23:59 UTC)
- **Blocker:** Milestones 2 & 3 evidence (live governance cycle, payment confirmation) still weak
- **Live link:** https://communityfund.stellar.org/programs/build-award

#### Tier 3: Grow Path (up to $500K)
The Grow path **unlocks only after mainnet launch**. Two sub-programs:

- **Marketing Grant** ($250K–$500K): For customer acquisition, brand, events, content
  - **Gate:** Live product on Stellar mainnet + evidence of traction (50+ active communities)
  - **Baraza status:** 🟡 Blocked until mainnet goes live
  
- **Matching Fund** ($250K–$500K): **Leverage math game-changer**
  - **How it works:** If you secure a lead investor for $1M, Stellar co-invests $500K, unlocking $2M total available
  - **Gate:** Lead investor commitment letter + Stellar approval
  - **Impact:** Cuts time to $1M+ by up to 6 months compared to sequential fundraising
  - **Baraza status:** 🟡 Contingent on lead investor interest; mainnet launch + Build Award payout strengthens positioning

### Why Mainnet Launch Matters

Stellar's funding ladder is **proof-gated**, not just capacity-gated:
1. **Instaward** needs idea + team credibility
2. **Build Award** needs working prototype + design validation
3. **Grow path** needs **live proof on mainnet** — one real community completing one real governance cycle

The $500K Marketing Grant is locked behind mainnet + 50-community traction. Until then, you're competing for Build Award and event sponsorship (ungated).

### Grant vs. Equity vs. Credits

BuildAfrica's funding mix will come from three sources with very different economics:

| Source Type | Example | Dilution | Timeline | Tax Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Grants (non-dilutive)** | SCF (Stellar), ANF, MDIF, Gitcoin, Mercy Corps | 0% | 6–12 weeks to payout | Tax-free in most jurisdictions (confirm with KRA) |
| **Equity (dilutive)** | VC round (Founders Factory, Circle Ventures, Polychain, etc.) | 10–25% per round | 3–6 months to close | Capital gains on sale; dividend-eligible if profitable |
| **Debt (dilutive via interest)** | MDIF Phase 2 (if pursued), Lend protocols | 0% founder dilution but interest accrues | 6–24 month term | Interest is expense; tax-deductible |
| **Credits (non-dilutive, infrastructure-only)** | AWS Accelerator, Anthropic credits, Google Cloud credits | 0% | Immediate | In-kind donation; not directly convertible to runway |

**BuildAfrica's mix (target $1M by Dec 2026):**
- Grants: $400K–$500K (grants + retro funding)
- Equity/seed: $250K–$300K (if VC round lands)
- Event + fund-operator revenue: $150K–$200K
- **Gap:** Explicitly model what fills shortfall; do not estimate to close
